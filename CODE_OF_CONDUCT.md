# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project should be a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Be considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels. Give technical feedback about the work, assume good intent, and focus on solving the problem.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and handled as appropriate to protect a welcoming project community.
